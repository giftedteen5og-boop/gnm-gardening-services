Problems We Solve

Every Gauteng garden faces unique challenges. Here's how G&M delivers real solutions.

Water Scarcity & Restrictions

The Problem: Gauteng's municipal water restrictions and unpredictable Highveld rainfall make it nearly impossible to maintain a lush garden year-round.

Our Solution: Water-wise garden design, drip irrigation, borehole & rainwater setups, and mulch layering that thrive on minimal water.

No Time for Maintenance

The Problem: Busy professionals, dual-income households, and working parents in Gauteng simply don't have weekends to mow, prune, and weed.

Our Solution: Affordable monthly and weekly maintenance plans — we handle everything so you can focus on what matters.

HOA & Estate Compliance

The Problem: Estates and HOAs in Lenasia, Ennerdale, and Johannesburg South face fines and complaints when gardens look untidy. Volunteers lack expertise and time.

Our Solution: Reliable commercial contracts with professional teams, safety compliance, and reporting to keep your community beautiful.

Poor Soil & Highveld Climate

The Problem: Highveld frost in winter, heavy summer storms, and clay-heavy Gauteng soil kill most non-native plants — wasting money and effort.

Our Solution: On-site soil testing, quality topsoil delivery, and climate-matched plant selection for gardens that last decades.

Safety & Trust Concerns

The Problem: Letting strangers onto your property is risky. Unvetted workers, no insurance, and unsafe equipment are common in Gauteng.

Our Solution: Fully insured, vetted, and trained teams. All our staff are background-checked and adhere to strict safety protocols.

Unaffordable Pricing

The Problem: Many landscaping companies in Gauteng overcharge or add hidden fees, making quality service a luxury only the wealthy can afford.

Our Solution: Transparent ballpark pricing via our instant estimate calculator, no hidden fees, and packages for every budget.

Social Impact

Uplifting Youth & Communities

G&M isn't just a gardening business — it's a youth-driven movement tackling South Africa's biggest crisis: unemployment. With over 45% of SA youth out of work — and even higher in Gauteng townships — we're building a different story, one garden at a time.

Jobs for Local Youth

We hire and train unemployed youth (18–35) from Ennerdale, Lenasia, and surrounding townships — creating sustainable, dignified work in a field with real growth potential.

Skills Development

Every team member receives hands-on training in horticulture, irrigation, landscaping, tree felling, rubble removal, and customer service — skills they keep for life.

Entrepreneurship Pipeline

Our goal: help each trained gardener start their own micro-business within 2 years. We mentor, supply starter toolkits, and refer overflow work.

Community Food Gardens

We donate 5% of our profits toward establishing food gardens at local schools, churches, and community centres — turning unused land into food security.

Elderly & Disability Support

We offer discounted garden services to pensioners and disabled community members who can no longer maintain their own gardens.

Local Supply Chain

We source plants and tools from local indigenous nurseries and township-based suppliers — keeping money circulating within our communities.
